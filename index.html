Every Story Has A Beginning

Roman Roberson’s journey began in San Diego, California. Growing up in foster care, later being adopted, and living on his own at just 15 shaped his values of resilience, independence, and grit.

Those experiences didn’t just teach him how to overcome. They fueled his passion for creating opportunities for others. That passion became the foundation of Tru Heart Consulting.

“I opened my business to give opportunity. I opened my business to change lives. I’m going to keep my promise.”

Roman Roberson, Tru Heart Instagram, Aug 1, 2025
